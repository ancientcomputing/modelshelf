# Getting help with Model Shelf

- **Help and common fixes:** https://thisbrain.ai/opensource/support.html#modelshelf
- **Email:** neuron@thisbrain.ai (put "Model Shelf" in the subject)
- **Bugs and feature requests:** [open an issue](https://github.com/ancientcomputing/modelshelf/issues)
- **Community:** [Discord](https://discord.gg/dydVBTe9Jq)

When you report a problem, include the app version (Model Shelf > About Model Shelf), your macOS
version, what you did, and the exact error message.
