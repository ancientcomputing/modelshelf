# Privacy

Model Shelf collects no data. It has no accounts, analytics or tracking, and it never sends anything
to ThisBrainAI LLC. It connects to the internet only when you choose Redownload, and then only to
Hugging Face.

The full policy, including where the app stores its activity log and how to delete it, is at
https://thisbrain.ai/opensource/privacy.html#modelshelf
